Cory

Colorful Illustration · Versatile Styles

Cory, a tattoo artist with 20 years of experience, joined I Love You Tattoo in April 2022.

He specializes in colorful illustrations and is versatile in different styles.

His main thing is creating cute, vibrant tattoos that last.

In his free time, he enjoys drawing with colored pencils, paint markers, and spray paint.

He has a lifelong passion for comics and cartoons and collects comics and action figures.

Cory's commitment to his craft is evident in every tattoo he does, making him a valuable team member.
