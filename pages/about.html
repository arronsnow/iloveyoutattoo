The Shop

143 Cherokee Heights Drive · Maryville, TN 37801

Built on love,
exceptional work & each other.

I Love You Tattoo was officially opened in March of 2021. Jenni envisioned a welcoming shop that would foster a strong reputation built on love, exceptional work, a positive attitude, and encouragement for all.

The relationship between our talented artists embodies all of these wonderful attributes! We continuously push each other to improve and help each other out whenever possible. Work truly doesn't feel like work when it's shared with your favorite people!

We genuinely enjoy hosting art nights, which are open to all artists in the community, where we can come together to paint, draw, and provide constructive criticism during our group critiques.

Additionally, our artists frequently travel to conventions and guest spots, expanding their skills and perspectives. We also make it a point to host guest artists whenever we have the opportunity!

We can't wait to make memories with you!
