Taking Care of Your Art

Tattoo Aftercare

Proper aftercare is the final step in getting a great tattoo. Follow these instructions to keep your new ink looking its best.

Days 1 - 3: Fresh Tattoo

  • Leave the bandage on for 2-4 hours (or as your artist directs)
  • Gently wash with unscented antibacterial soap and warm water
  • Pat dry with a clean paper towel - do not rub
  • Apply a thin layer of unscented lotion (Lubriderm, Aveeno)
  • Repeat washing and moisturizing 2-3 times per day
  • Keep out of direct sunlight and avoid soaking in water

Days 4 - 14: Healing Phase

  • Your tattoo will begin to peel - this is completely normal
  • Do NOT pick, scratch, or peel flaking skin
  • Continue moisturizing 2x per day with unscented lotion
  • Avoid swimming pools, hot tubs, and long showers
  • Wear loose, breathable clothing over the tattoo
  • Stay out of direct sun - no sunbathing or tanning beds

Long Term Care

  • Always apply SPF 30+ sunscreen over healed tattoos in the sun
  • Keep skin moisturized to maintain color vibrancy
  • Avoid prolonged sun exposure which causes fading
  • Stay hydrated - healthy skin = healthy tattoos

Signs to Watch For

  • Some redness and swelling in the first 24-48 hours is normal
  • Contact us if you notice excessive swelling, pus, or fever
  • Itching is normal - tap gently, never scratch
  • When in doubt, call or DM us - we're always happy to help
